# v17 Flightweight Audit

- Printed structure: 274 g estimated from CAD volumes
- PLA: 188.2 cm3 -> 233.3 g
- PETG: 32.0 cm3 -> 40.7 g
- P2S fit: 34 / 34 parts
- Battery support: 3S 1500-2200 mAh
- 3S 2200 endurance configuration remains supported
- Target propulsion: SunnySky X2212 2450KV, 6x4, 40A ESC
- Static thrust reference: ~880 g on 3S / 6x4
- Estimated AUW with 3S 2200: approximately 610-650 g depending on actual battery, wiring, receiver and hardware
- Corresponding static thrust / weight: approximately 1.35-1.44
- Estimated AUW with 3S 1500-1800: approximately 560-610 g
- Corresponding static thrust / weight: approximately 1.44-1.57

## Structural rules

The reduced mass is achieved without changing Final Master exterior geometry. Sponson and center-body shells use 0.8 mm nominal shell thickness, canopy is hollow, lifting/control panels are reduced to 2.0 mm where appropriate, fins/rudders to 1.6 mm, and PETG skids to 1.5 mm. Carbon reinforcement and local PETG inserts are required at high-load paths before physical release.

## Release gate

This remains a CAD engineering estimate. Final flight release requires printed mass, actual CG, wattmeter thrust/current test, servo brownout test, flotation/planing test, grass/sand ground test and flight test.
